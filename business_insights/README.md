
# Sales Analytics — Business Insights & Recommendations

## Project Overview
This analysis evaluates 1,000 sales transactions
from 2025 to identify sales trends, profitability,
regional performance, and business opportunities.

## Key Performance Indicators

| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| Total Sales | INR 1,56,42,337.76 |
| Total Profit | INR 39,05,214.76 |
| Total Orders | 1,000 |
| Total Quantity Sold | 2,067 |

## Key Business Insights

### 1. Monthly Sales Performance
- October 2025 recorded the highest sales at INR 18.58 lakh.
- June 2025 recorded the lowest sales at INR 9.01 lakh.

### 2. Regional Performance
- The West region generated the highest sales,
  approximately INR 37 lakh.

### 3. Category Profitability
- Electronics was the most profitable category,
  generating approximately INR 32.33 lakh in profit.

### 4. Sales Channel Performance
- Online sales: INR 78.72 lakh across 499 orders.
- In-store sales: INR 77.70 lakh across 501 orders.
- Both channels contributed almost equally to revenue.

## Business Recommendations

1. Investigate the reasons behind June's lower sales
   and test targeted promotional campaigns.

2. Study the West region's sales performance and
   identify strategies applicable to other regions.

3. Prioritize high-profit Electronics products while
   monitoring inventory and demand.

4. Continue investing in both online and in-store
   channels and compare their profitability.

## Conclusion
The analysis highlights monthly sales fluctuations,
regional differences, and category-level profitability.
These insights can support better inventory planning,
marketing strategies, and data-driven decisions.
